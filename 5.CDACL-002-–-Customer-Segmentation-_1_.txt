CDACL-002 – Customer Segmentation
Customer Segmentation and Shopping Trend Analysis Using SQL,
Power BI and Orange Data Mining



Project Overview
This project analyzes the shopping behavior of 3,900 customers from a U.S. retail shopping dataset. The
main goal is to understand customer purchasing patterns, identify which products need discounts, study
spending based on age, payment methods, seasons, and locations, and group customers into similar
categories using K-Means Clustering. The results help businesses improve sales, customer satisfaction,
and marketing strategies.




Objectives
     • Analyze customer shopping behavior.
     • Find product categories where discounts should be applied.
     • Study spending based on age and payment method.
     • Analyze seasonal and location-wise sales.
     • Segment customers into 3 groups using K-Means.
     • Recommend marketing campaigns for each customer group.




Tools Used
     • MySQL – Data Analysis using SQL
     • Power BI – Dashboard and Visualization
     • Orange Data Mining – Customer Segmentation

Customer Segmentation (K-Means)
K-Means is a Machine Learning algorithm used to group customers with similar shopping behavior.


Features Used

     • Age
     • Purchase Amount
     • Previous Purchases
     • Review Rating

How K-Means Works

    1. Load the shopping dataset.
    2. Select important customer features.
    3. Normalize the data.
    4. Choose K = 3 clusters.




                                                    2

    5. Randomly create 3 cluster centers (centroids).

    6. Assign each customer to the nearest cluster.
    7. Update the cluster centers.
    8. Repeat until the clusters no longer change.
    9. The final result is 3 customer groups.

Customer Clusters
Cluster 1 – Budget Customers

     • Low spending
     • Use discounts frequently
     • Buy low-cost products

Marketing: Coupons, flash sales, and bundle offers.


Cluster 2 – Premium Customers

     • High spending
     • Loyal customers
     • Frequent purchases

Marketing: VIP membership, reward points, and personalized offers.




                                                        3

Cluster 3 – Seasonal Customers

     • Shop mainly during festivals or special seasons
     • Medium spending
     • Use promotional offers

Marketing: Festival discounts, seasonal offers, and free shipping.
